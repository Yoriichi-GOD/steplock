# Technical limitations and open evidence gates

**REAL_STAGED_DATA: NOT_PROVIDED. Physical webcam operation: NOT_TESTED.** Transport
tests used generated JPEGs; the scored corpus is generated video. No factory,
hardware safety, worker performance, fatigue or accident-prevention validity follows.

- Tags identify labels; swapped/copied labels break intrinsic-part identity.
- Visible placement is not fastening, torque, contact or structural integrity.
  Illustrations are not reconstructed geometry or inspection evidence.
- Marker centers define the coordinate frame. No calibrated 3D pose, lens correction,
  metric millimeter tolerance, hand recognition or action understanding is claimed.
- Bright uniform white-board insets provide constrained emptiness evidence. A white
  occluder or hidden stacked object can defeat this premise. Unobserved is not absent.
- Removal needs supported clear space, replacement or unique-tag relocation. Manual
  four-point calibration expires after 1800ms without visible anchors.
- Distinct images/time do not cryptographically prove independent camera exposures.
  A near-duplicate heuristic catches the measured MJPEG case; edited copies can evade
  it and clean stationary frames may abstain. Exact/near repeats cannot add support.
- Midassembly starts cannot prove order. Adding a prerequisite after an early cover
  does not retroactively validate the cover; remove/re-place it in order.
- Generated clips share a renderer, recipe and finite transform groups. Scores and
  Wilson intervals are descriptive, not independent industrial population estimates.
- Vision processing timing excludes browser rendering. Offline throughput includes
  three engines/logging. True capture-to-visible latency and idle/active CPU overhead
  were NOT_MEASURED. No GPU benchmark or fabricated cost claim.
- Assembly101 is inventory-only; no justified toy-action mapping. HoloAssist/IKEA ASM
  training/evaluation NOT_RUN. No public-dataset visual score is claimed.
- One active source per process. Sessions retain files but do not resume engine state
  after process restart. No tamper-proof ledger, multi-machine deployment review or
  certified safety software. A local filesystem owner can edit evidence files.

Raw evidence stays local and ignored by Git. Camera permission and consent remain
human responsibilities; publishing footage needs separate redistribution consent.
No audio, face ID, attendance or staff evaluation is performed.
