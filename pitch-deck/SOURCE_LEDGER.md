## External research and limitations

The ASUSE PDF was downloaded from MoSPI (17,207,885 bytes) and the cited tables
read directly with pypdf. The survey is not a directory of assembly factories or
a manufacturing-SME sales database. No SAM/SOM or eligible-station fraction is
inferred from it. Registration totals and old NSS 2015–16 estimates were not mixed
with this newer survey. RAMP supports broad capability constraints, not a claim
that no incumbent serves small factories. Price, setup time and willingness to pay
remain unknown. No competitor pricing or comparative performance was invented.

Sequoia's [business-plan guidance](https://sequoiacap.com/article/writing-a-business-plan)
informed coverage of purpose, problem, solution, market, alternatives, business
model, team and vision. The iPhone keynote is a narrative reference supplied by
the user; no Apple footage, logo, screenshot or quotation was reused.

The [official Speedrun FAQ](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq) was checked on 2 Nov
2026. Its stated SR008 deadline is 1 Nov at 11:59 PM PT (2 Nov 13:29 IST).
No application submission or acceptance was performed or claimed. Program dates,
investment terms and endorsement claims are not used to sell STEPLOCK.
